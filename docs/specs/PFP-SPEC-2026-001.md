> > ## 1. What PFP Is
> >
> > Three upstream projects unified under one institutional-grade specification:
> >
> > - Venues: registered namespace-scoped data-flow origins
> > - - Fresco Binding Layer: validates frames, enforces sequence monotonicity, maintains lineage chain, issues Commitment Tokens
> >   - - Preston Governance Kernel: policy assertion engine, session management, KernelManifest issuance
> >    
> >     - Reference implementation: Go 1.22. OpenClaw Colony = Python 3.11 secondary binding layer.
> >    
> >     - ---

> > ## 3. Invariant Gap Analysis

> > | ID | Definition | Status |

> > | INV-001 | Frame must reference ACTIVE BindingContract | Partial |
> > | INV-002 | sequence_number strictly monotonic no gaps | Partial |

> > | INV-004 | LineageNode hash = SHA-256(parent_hash concat payload_digest) | Satisfied |
> > | INV-005 | Root LineageNode recorded in KernelManifest | Partial |
> > | INV-006 | KernelManifest immutable after issuance | Not Implemented |

> > | INV-009 | BindingContract only for ACTIVE Venues | Not Implemented |
> > | INV-010 | Commitment Tokens globally unique | Not Implemented |
> > | INV-011 | Namespace strings globally unique | Partial |
> > | INV-012 | KernelManifest policy_assertions non-empty | Partial |
> > | INV-013 | Session Anchor hash written before OpenSessionResponse | Not Implemented |
> > | INV-014 | mTLS enforced on all inter-service channels | Not Implemented |
> > | INV-015 | Audit Epoch IDs strictly monotonically increasing | Not Implemented |
> > | INV-016 | Session replay detected via Anchor Registry | Not Implemented |
