## The Economics Of Giving

The MANNA split is 84 / 15 / 1.

84% to the community pool.
15% to the crew who did the work.
1% to the architect.

Notice what is not in that split.

There is no extraction line.
There is no investor line.
There is no platform fee line.
There is no rent line.

The value generated by the community
stays in the community.

That is not charity.
That is not ideology.
That is mathematics applied to justice.

**When the people who create the value
are the same people who receive the value,
the system is self-sustaining.**

When they are not,
the system is a pump
moving wealth from the many to the few.

OpenClaw Colony is not a pump.
